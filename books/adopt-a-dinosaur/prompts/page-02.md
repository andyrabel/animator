# Tyrannosaurus Rex

The Tyrannosaurus rex stays where he is.
He sways slowly and gently from side to side inside the house.
Its tiny arms wriggling with a little wobble. 
It blinks its large eyes once, slowly.
It turns towards the viewer and roars loudly so we see his big jaws full of teath!
Items on a nearby shelf wobble gently from the vibration.
