# Diplodocus (Dippie & Dot)

Dippie the large green Diplodocus in the foreground sways her 
very long neck slowly from side to side, 
completely blocking the view of the TV behind her. 
The TV is turned on, but we can't see what the show is - maybe it's animals.
Dot the smaller Diplodocus behind tilts her head curiously. 
Both blink their large dark eyes in turn. 
The living room furniture stays perfectly still.
