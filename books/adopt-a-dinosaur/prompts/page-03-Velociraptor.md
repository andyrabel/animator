# Velociraptor

The Velociraptor's large yellow eye darts left then right with a clever, 
mischievous glint, scanning the room. 
It tilts its head slowly to one side. 
One clawed foot taps the floor once, impatiently. 
The potted plants on the windowsill tremble slightly.
It sees something small, catches it, and gobbles it up quickly.
