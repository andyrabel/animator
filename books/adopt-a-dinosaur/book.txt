Adopt a Dinosaur, by Andrew Abel

Page 00 - Front Cover (Stegosaurus)

Page 01 - Welcome
Welcome to the Dino Adoption Agency
We hope you find a Dino you can love!

Page 02 - Tyrannosaurus rex
Tyrannosaurus rex, is grand,
But not a pet, you'll understand.
With dagger teeth and hunger vast,
Your living room just wouldn't last.

Page 03 - Velociraptor
Velociraptor - smart and sly,
But not a pet, I'll tell you why.
They'd look around for some way out,
And soon escape without a doubt.

Page 04 - Triceratops
Triceratops, has three sharp horns,
They'd like to eat your neighbor's lawns.
They'd charge around and break your toys
And surely make a lot of noise!

Page 05 - Brachiosaurus
Brachiosaurus, towering high,
As a pet, I wouldn't try.
They'd bellow loud and stand aloof.
And push their head right through the roof!

Page 06 - Stegosaurus
Stegosaurus - plated spine.
They'd never make a pet of mine.
Their spikey tail would swish about,
And smash the doors and windows out.

Page 07 - Diplodocus
Diplodocus - long, long neck,
Your bedroom would be quite a wreck!
And on your lap they wouldn't sit.
You'd never get to cuddle it.

Page 08 - Pteranodon
Pteranodon, flies in the sky,
A pet it's not, you should know why.
They'd flap their wings and smash the light
No room to fly, it's just not right.

Page 09 - Ankylosaurus
Ankylosaurus, built so strong,
But as a pet, it would be wrong.
Their club-like tail would cause a fuss,
I'd have to give a "no" from us.

Page 10 - Closing
Dinosaurs are great to see.,
But not as pets, just let them be.
On ancient earth they used to roam.
They should not live inside our home.
