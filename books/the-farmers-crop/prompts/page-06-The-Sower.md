# The Sower

> For it's God that wants His life to grow,
> And calls us all to sow, sow, sow.
> With childlike faith and patience slow,
> To plant a seed and watch it grow,
> And share His love with those we know.

Use the uploaded illustration as the starting frame. Preserve the original hand-carved black-and-white linocut exactly — same bold relief-print shapes, carved gouge marks, stark high contrast, proportions, and composition. Pure black ink on white paper, no colour and no grey shading. Do not redraw or reinterpret the print. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom, tilt, or cuts.

The sower strides slowly forward across the ploughed field with an unhurried, rocking gait. His forward hand sweeps in a broad, steady arc and opens; a fan of carved seed flies out and scatters across the dark furrows, then his hand returns to the bag at his waist and gathers another handful. His smock and trouser folds shift with each step; the broad hat stays level.

The long carved furrow-lines converge toward the low sun on the horizon. The sun glows steady while the bold swirling bands of the sky wheel very slowly around it. Cast seed keeps falling in soft showers onto the near ground and settling into the carved soil.

End on the same composition, the sower mid-stride with a fresh arc of seed leaving his hand.

**Sound:** slow footsteps on turned earth, the soft swish of a swinging arm, the scatter and patter of falling seed, a light wind over the field, distant birdsong. No dialogue, narration, singing, or music.

**Style:** faithful to the original linocut / woodblock relief print, bold black-and-white folk-art woodcut aesthetic, subtle print-like movement only. No new characters, no colour, no added shading, no camera moves. Movement stays slow, rhythmic, and hopeful — the patient work of sowing.
