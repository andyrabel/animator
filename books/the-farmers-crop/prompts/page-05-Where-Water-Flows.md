# Where Water Flows

> So heed the word that the Father sows.
> Listen close to the One who knows.
> In softened hearts the seedling grows.
> Not caught and choked by wealth or woes,
> It drinks in deep where water flows.

Use the uploaded illustration as the starting frame. Preserve the original hand-carved black-and-white linocut exactly — same bold relief-print shapes, carved gouge marks, stark high contrast, proportions, and composition. Pure black ink on white paper, no colour and no grey shading. Do not redraw or reinterpret the print. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom, tilt, or cuts.

The row of young carved seedlings along the bank sways gently and evenly in a soft breeze, their feathery ears nodding, standing tall and unhurried. New growth seems to rise in them, the shoots lengthening a touch toward the sky.

Below, the wide band of carved water flows steadily across the frame in slow curling lines, its little spiral eddies turning. The beaded roots of the seedlings reach down into the current and drink, pulsing faintly as the water moves past them. The smooth carved stones in the stream sit fast while the water parts around them.

The horizontal bands of the sky above drift slowly and calmly.

End on the same peaceful composition, the seedlings drinking deep as the water flows on.
