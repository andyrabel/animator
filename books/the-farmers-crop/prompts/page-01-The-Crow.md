# The Crow

> The farmer wants his crops to grow.
> He enters the field to sow, sow, sow.
> And spreads the seed wherever it can go.
> But a black bird swoops in low, low, low,
> And the seed's carried off by a big fat crow.

Pure black ink on white paper, no colour and no grey shading. Do not redraw or reinterpret the print. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom, tilt, or cuts.

Begin with a few carved corn seeds scattering down from off the top of the frame — flung by the unseen sower — arcing through the air and landing among the grain already on the ground, skittering a little and settling. The seeds are cut in the same bold linocut style, pure black on white. Then the action begins.

The big fat crow fills the foreground, head bowed. It gives slow, deliberate pecks at the scattered grain on the ground, dipping its heavy beak and lifting its head between pecks, throat feathers shifting. One seed is snatched up and held fast in its beak as the crow raises its head. Its glossy back and folded wings ruffle slightly, the carved white flecks flickering like a fresh print. Its clawed feet make small shuffling steps among the seeds.

Having finished pecking, the crow spreads its heavy carved wings, gives one or two slow downbeats, and lifts off the ground, rising and carrying the stolen seed with it as it flies out of the frame. Only the crow moves in this way — it leaves behind the same stark field of scattered seed.

Across the upper sky the loose flock of small carved birds flaps slowly onward, rising and drifting, a few new birds swooping in low from the edge of the frame. The scattered seeds on the ground stay put but for the few the crow disturbs. The ragged black border stays perfectly still, like the edge of an inked block.

End on the stark field with the crow gone from frame, the last seed carried off, the small flock still drifting across the upper sky.

**Sound:** a light patter of scattered seed landing on the ground at the open, then a rough caw from the crow, the dry rustle and tick of grain, the slow heavy flap of wings overhead, a thin wind across an open field. No dialogue, narration, singing, or music.

**Style:** faithful to the original linocut / woodblock relief print, bold black-and-white folk-art woodcut aesthetic, subtle print-like movement only. No new creatures or objects, no colour, no added shading, no camera moves. Movement stays slow, weighty, and deliberate.
