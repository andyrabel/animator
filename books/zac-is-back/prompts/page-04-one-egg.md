# One Egg

> He asked for an egg, in a nice polite way.
> "Sure," I replied. "I just bought some today."
> So I gave him an egg and he laughed with delight.
> And so he ran off and was out of my sight.

**Starting frame:** the uploaded clean plate (all page lettering removed).

Use the clean plate exactly as the first frame. Zac — the fuzzy blue puppet with
round glasses, pink nose and striped shirt — leans in past a loose black-ink
line-art open door on the right, both hands raised, cradling a single brown
photographic egg in one hand, thrilled. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom,
tilt, or cuts.

Zac lifts the egg a little higher and turns it gently, his eyes going wide with
delight. He gives a bouncing, silent laugh — fur shaking, shoulders hopping —
then begins to lean back as if about to dash away, glancing off-frame. The
line-art door outline flexes like a live sketch. End with Zac mid-turn, the egg
held close, grinning back over his shoulder.

The egg stays photographic and solid; do not stylise it. Do not render any text,
letters, numbers, or captions anywhere in the frame.

**Sound:** a happy gasp and a giggle, the light tick of a fingernail on
eggshell, quick soft footsteps trailing away. No dialogue, narration, singing,
or music.

**Style:** keep the delivered mixed-media look intact — soft 3D Zac, black-ink
line-art door, photographic egg, none of them blended or restyled. Static
camera, quick but light motion.
