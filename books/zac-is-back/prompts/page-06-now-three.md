# This Time He Wants Three

> A little while later he's back ringing the bell.
> What was he up to? I just couldn't tell.
> Again it was Zac, won't he just let me be?
> Now I'm giving him eggs— this time he wants . . . three!
> So I turned off the doorbell!

**Starting frame:** the uploaded clean plate (all page lettering removed).

Use the clean plate exactly as the first frame. Zac — the fuzzy blue puppet with
round glasses, pink nose and striped shirt — is large on the left, grinning
wide, one blue hand raised showing three fingers. Three brown photographic eggs
run across the lower right. Loose black-ink line-art window frames are behind
him. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom, tilt, or cuts.

Zac holds up his three fingers and gives them a small insistent wiggle, eyebrows
lifting into an eager, slightly cheeky grin. He blinks, fur ruffling, and leans
in a touch toward the viewer. The three eggs rest still. The line-art behind him
shimmers faintly like a living sketch. End on three fingers up and a big
hopeful grin.

The eggs stay photographic and solid. Do not render any text, letters, numbers,
or captions anywhere in the frame.

**Sound:** a repeated doorbell chime that abruptly cuts to dead silence, a
sheepish little hum, a soft rustle of fur. No dialogue, narration, singing, or
music.

**Style:** keep the delivered mixed-media look intact — soft 3D Zac, black-ink
line-art room, photographic eggs, never blended or restyled. Static camera,
gentle motion only.
