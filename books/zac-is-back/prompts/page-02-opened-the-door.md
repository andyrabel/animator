# Opened the Door

> I opened the door and who should I meet . . .

**Starting frame:** the uploaded clean plate (all page lettering removed).

Use the clean plate exactly as the first frame. Loose hand-drawn black-ink
line-art on plain white — a panelled front door swung part-way open on the left,
a curtained window and a small potted plant on the right. Through the gap of the
open door, a single bright-blue furry hand and wrist reach in, a blue-and-white
striped cuff at the wrist. Camera completely static — no pan, zoom, tilt, or
cuts.

The door drifts open a few more degrees, its ink outline flexing like a live
sketch. The blue furry hand spreads its fingers, gives a small friendly wave,
then the fingers curl and relax. The curtains and the potted-plant leaves sway
gently. Every black line breathes with a faint hand-drawn wobble. End with the
blue hand open, mid friendly wave, just inside the doorway.

Only the hand and striped cuff are visible of the visitor — no face, no body
yet. Do not render any text, letters, numbers, or captions anywhere in the
frame.

**Sound:** the creak of a door hinge, a light knock fading, quiet room tone.
No dialogue, narration, singing, or music.

**Style:** keep the delivered look intact — loose black-ink line-art room, the
one soft-rendered blue furry hand, nothing repainted or restyled, no blending of
the two looks. Static camera, slow subtle motion only.
