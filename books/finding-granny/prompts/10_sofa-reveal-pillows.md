# I Think It's the Orange One — Room for Three

The boy kneels on the carpet, pointing eagerly at the big orange three-seater sofa with its two purple patterned cushions, his pointing arm bouncing slightly for emphasis as he rocks forward on his knees, grinning at the sofa. Beside him, the girl leans over the back of the sofa, grabbing one purple cushion in each hand and lifting them up to peek underneath, wobbling slightly with the effort as her ponytail swings forward over her shoulder. A dropped clue drawing sits on the floor below, corner fluttering slightly.
